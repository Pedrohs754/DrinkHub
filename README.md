🍸 DrinkHub

Discover. Mix. Enjoy.

DrinkHub is a digital cocktail catalog designed to help users discover, explore, and prepare drinks. Featuring a collection of over 500 cocktail recipes, intuitive filters, signature creations, and practical tools for organizing ingredients and shopping lists.

✨ Features

🍹 Cocktail Catalog

* Explore a collection of 500+ cocktail recipes.
* Browse detailed information about each drink.
* View ingredients, measurements, and preparation instructions.
* Discover different types and categories of cocktails.

🔎 Advanced Filters

* Filter cocktails by category.
* Search by main alcoholic beverage.
* Find drinks based on specific ingredients.
* Explore cocktails according to different characteristics.

⭐ Signature Cocktails

* Discover original cocktail creations.
* Explore exclusive recipes developed for DrinkHub.

🛒 Shopping List

* Add beverages and ingredients to your shopping list.
* Organize items needed to prepare selected cocktails.
* Manage your shopping list in one convenient place.

🥄 Bar Tools

* Explore essential bar utensils and equipment.
* Discover the tools used in cocktail preparation.

🎯 Project Purpose

DrinkHub aims to make cocktail discovery and preparation more accessible, organized, and enjoyable.

Whether exploring classic cocktails, discovering new combinations, or organizing ingredients for the next gathering, DrinkHub brings everything together in one platform.
